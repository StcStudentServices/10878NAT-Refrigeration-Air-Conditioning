    7. Book Your Theory Assessment

    All students must follow the conditions below during their theory assessment. Failure to follow these requirements may result in the assessment being stopped, rescheduled or marked as invalid.

    Assessment Supervision Requirements

    • Your screen must be shared at all times during the assessment.
    • Your camera must remain switched on so the supervisor can see you throughout the assessment.
    • Your camera must be positioned so the supervisor can clearly see your face, hands and workspace.
    • Your microphone must remain switched on so the supervisor can hear you if you speak.
    • At the beginning of the session, you may be asked to briefly show your room and surroundings using your camera.

    Assessment Environment

    • You must complete the assessment in a quiet room by yourself.
    • No other person is permitted to be in the room during the assessment.
    • Notes, mobile phones and unauthorised materials are not permitted.
    • A calculator is permitted unless otherwise advised by the trainer.

    Suspicious Activity Monitoring

    Energy Space includes a Suspicious Activity monitoring feature.

    If you open another browser tab, leave the assessment screen or access unauthorised materials, the system may detect the activity. The assessment may be stopped and marked as Not Satisfactory.

    This will count as an assessment attempt and you may be required to reschedule.

    Assessment Attempts

    Students are permitted a maximum of two attempts per assessment, including one resit.

    Additional attempts may incur fees. Please refer to your Letter of Offer and Student Handbook for further information.

    9. Portfolio Documentation - End of qualification

    The End of Qualification Documentation is the final and essential stage of your qualification process.

    At this stage, you are required to submit a complete set of documents to verify your work experience, licensing status, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

    This documentation allows us to assess your eligibility for completion and certification.

    Please ensure that all forms are completed accurately and that all supporting evidence is current and valid

    All submitted documentation must be current and validated within the last three (3) months. Documents older than this period may not be accepted and may require re-issue or re-verification.

    Document Requirement Details
    Provisional ARC Licence Must be valid
    Overseas Employer Reference Evidence of AC work experience
    Australian Employer Reference(s) Minimum of 12 months experience
    Referee Testimonials (x2) From licensed AC in Australia (form attached) Reference letters must include:
    • Company letterhead/logo and contact details
    • Employer name, signature, and license number
    • Duration of employment, role, and industry activities
    • Detailed scope of AC work undertaken
    CPR Certificate HLTAID009 – issued within the last 12 months
    Updated Resume Current and up to date
    Employment History Form Form attached
    Self-Assessment Questionnaire Form attached

    Incomplete or outdated documentation may delay the processing of your qualification. If you have any questions or require clarification, please contact our administration team before submitting your documents.
